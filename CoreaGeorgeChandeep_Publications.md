### 2026

- **[Learning Wherobots by Building a National AI Data Center Suitability Report](https://wherobots.com/blog/ai-data-center-site-suitability-wherobots/)** — Invited technical blog article, Wherobots platform. 27 August 2026. Featured on Wherobots corporate blog; demonstrates cloud spatial SQL for national infrastructure siting analysis.

- **What happens when you ask an AI agent to build a national spatial pipeline?** — Presentation. FunGIS 2026 Conference ("Past, Present, Future" 35th Anniversary). LinkedIn article. Examines AI hallucinations in statutory GIS and presents the Human-AI Triad architecture: (1) Orchestrator (human intent), (2) AI Engine (translation), (3) QA Gate (safety valve). Discusses anti-mock safeguards including live API reconciliation, deep payload inspection, GeoLibre QA Inspector, and dry-run airlocks for eliminating fake data in national spatial pipelines.

- **FunGIS Spatial Olympics 2026** — Operational workflows and field app sync troubleshooting guide for outdoor spatial competition. Documents QField and mobile application workflows for team coordination and photo submission management. Co-lead organizer. Created 13 August 2026; updated 14 August 2026.

- **Direct QGIS Edits on Esri Geodatabase** — Technical documentation. Outlines critical failure modes, specifically ObjectID desynchronization traps, encountered when executing direct QGIS inserts on unversioned Esri SQL Server databases. Proposes dual-role architecture to enforce data integrity and prevent corruption in enterprise GIS environments. Created 1 September 2026; updated 1 September 2026.

- **The_Dual_Engine_GIS_Blueprint.pptx** — Presentation outline. References core HTML index structural elements and dual-role architectural patterns for resilient spatial systems. Created 26 August 2026; updated 26 August 2026.

- **Untitled presentation** — Quick access reference for FunGIS Spatial Olympics 2026 photo submission workflow. Created 13 August 2026; updated 13 August 2026.

- **JobHunt Crafter AI: LinkedIn Presentation** — Introduces context-driven pipeline framework for storing milestone data, mapping job search stages, and converting unstructured thoughts into STAR interview responses. Created and updated 1 June 2026.

### 2025

- **Intro to FOSS4G** — Workshop presentation. Introduces open-source geospatial architectures (QGIS, PostGIS, GeoServer), print composition workflows, and available regional training directories. Created 24 September 2025; updated 3 October 2025.

- **AIRS-SLATS-QGIS-Workflow_CoreaGC_Worktask** — Technical documentation. Documents the structural modernization framework ("Deploy Aid") that evolved a legacy, crash-prone ERDAS workflow into a single, cohesive QGIS ecosystem with automated audit trail for SLATS change detection. Demonstrates migration from proprietary remote sensing workflows to open-source geospatial stack. Created 30 June 2025; updated 12 July 2026.

- **SLATS_Deploy_Aid_v2.pptx** — User manual for deploying custom QGIS VMR plugins (Flicker, ReCode, Project Manager), detailing steps to structure raster environments and audit clearing data for change detection workflows. Created 29 June 2025; updated 26 June 2025.

- **X_Your_Code.pptx** — Examines iterative logic development workflows utilizing ChatGPT as an interactive junior programmer. Case study: writing and refining custom smoothing algorithms for profiling Malleefowl nests. Demonstrates AI-assisted development for specialized ecological analysis. Created 25 June 2025; updated 25 June 2024.
